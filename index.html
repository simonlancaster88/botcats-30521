About

A robotics team built to compete.

BotCats is a FIRST Tech Challenge team out of Union Middle School in San Jose, drawing on Silicon Valley's talent pool to design, build, and program a competition robot from scratch each season. We're currently deep into the 2026–2027 BIOBUZZ season, iterating on our robot's mechanical design and driver-station software ahead of the fall qualifiers.

We're a hands-on team: students own CAD, wiring, and code, with mentors guiding strategy and technical review rather than doing the work for them.
